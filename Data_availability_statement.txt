DATA AND SOFTWARE AVAILABILITY

The R package spinet (v0.1.0, GPL-3), which implements all methods described
in this paper, is archived at Zenodo (DOI: TO BE ADDED ON RELEASE) and
developed at https://github.com/ehsanrahimi666/spinet.

Species distribution models for 187 Chilean plants and 171 pollinators
(present and 2070 SSP5-8.5), the documented metaweb, the phenology derived
from dated interaction records, all simulation outputs and every figure script
are archived at Zenodo (DOI: TO BE ADDED ON RELEASE).

Interaction records are from Muschett & Fonturbel (2022), openly available at
https://github.com/fonturbel-lab/pollination_catalogue.

Occurrence records were obtained from GBIF. Climate data are from WorldClim 2.1.

No new empirical data were collected for this study.
